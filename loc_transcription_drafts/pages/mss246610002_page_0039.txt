54 Mr. North Naval Officer at Boston called yesterday
55 and again this Am. while here he said he
56 content to know if he could accept a
57 post which might be sent him by B.H.A. Navy
58 of wh. he had been everwell for some years.
59 I told him certainly not & that on my
60 opinion he would not consistently remain
61 counsel of any Mr. or other officer or
62 under. directly or indirectly having to do
63 with customs. I added that a new regulation
64 could directly or indirectly on this subject &
65 advised him to resign as Atty. He said he
66 would do this at once. I then discussed
67 a regulation to be framed covering this whole
