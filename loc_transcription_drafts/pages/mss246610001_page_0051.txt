1894 Nov 22 Mr. Holmes of Bost. Herald wrote me today
that the interview alluded to supra p. 98 was
abbreviated because of "lack of space". He
sent his note to City Editor & his endorsement
on back of it giving reasons for abbreviation.
It is still my opinion that Boverus
deliberately suppressed it.
While in Boston -- on election day last --
Henry R. Rud told me he had given
$5000.00 to secure the nomination of Pol
C'Nail for Congress and the defeat of
Fitzgerald who finally secured the
nomination. In my judgment this
must have contributed to the
Sugar Trust; Rud gave only $200 to
our campaign fund -- said he would
have given more had not the Irving
Men's Club tried to cut his
throat in resolution favoring abolition
of discriminating duties on refined
sugar.
