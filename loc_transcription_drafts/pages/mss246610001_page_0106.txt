19 Sunday. In Kobe, the Sun & setting
Evening, had a man to meet me at
train with a carriage belonging to Mr
Ent. Called later to leave us to Fisher's
Exhibition. In P. M. went to Aquarium
by boat, left for Kobe.
20 Kioto. Kioto hotel.
Visited imperial garden and also out
of the castles of the Shoguns. Visited a
silver weaving establishment. Met Charlotte
Bowdilet w. 2 friends Miss Baylor and
Mr. Taintor. Bought very old (300 years)
Koran bowl for 125 Yen and gave it to
Sir William Van Horn.

Tuesday. Wednesday.

Yosio P. M.
Called w. Baron Kitamura on Marquis
Isle at residence of Count Suyouge.
Met Count Suyouge daughter. Most beautiful
house - showed us all over it.
Marquis Isle assured me again and
again that no matter what course
U.S. took as to Hawaii then would be
no trouble bit. Japan Y.M.S. - that
