July 3
Left afternoon train for Boston. Took

July 4
Called on Mrs Bryan 13 Els St. She
took me over the cathedral etc
1 P.M. left for Chicago.
July 5
Am. Chicago 7.30.
Attended convention. The delegates
were monomaniacs. Hine did every
efficient work for the sound money
man. Ed. Gnd Williams voted & won
for the five silver men in defiance
of his instructions, I considered the
five silver plank as not the worst
thing the Convention did. The attack
upon the President for ordering the
N.S. troops to put down the N.R.
nots would have made me bolt
the Convention if there had been a
gold plank in the platform.
July 9
Left Chicago. The platform had been
announced. And there was nothing
now to be done -- no respectable man
could afford to remain.

would drive over & see the President.
He said he thought the President ought
to be left alone. That he did not care

know something about the Convention
