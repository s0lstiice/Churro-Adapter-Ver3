2.30 P.M. First meeting of the Fur
Seal Whistle conference between U.S.
and Great Britain. Dr. Jordan & Mr.
the U.S. and Prof. D'Arey Thompson
and Mr. Macoun for Britain & Canada.
It was stipulated by Great Britain
that it should be a conference of
experts only but later it was agreed
to broaden the scope so that the
Diplomatic representation would
be present and participate to the
extent of assuring questions, at least,
in the meeting.
Sir Wilfrid Laurier, Prince Minister
of Canada, Sir Louis Davis, Min.
of Marine and Fisheries, & Lds Julian
Pannelford signified their intention
of taking part in the Conference.
The meeting organized by closing
up on motion of Prof. Thompson,
presiding officer, and appointing
Mr. Clark of San F. as the American
& Mr. Vernon of Canada as the
Brit. Sec'y.
It was voted that the deliberation
should be secret. The Amcr. delegates
presented a printed paper stating
certain facts they wished to be

found as stated by the convention.
Convention met at 11 am. Canada & Amer.
experts & Secy's present -- also Foster, Davis
and Adam Chargé d'Affairs. First bus.
was setting form of protocol. Macoun
claimed that our statement of fact
presented at last meeting should form
no part officially of the protocol. He
finally ruled -- subject to the vote of
the convention -- that as the printed
statement of facts was officially
offered by the Amer. delegates it formed
a part of the official records and
should be included in the Protocol.
Finally the objection was withdrawn
and the Protocol approved.
We next stated that Jordan's draft of a report had been
given to Davis -- that all this was asked
but East for Macouns Report for 1896
it was an inadvertence only the
report for 1896 being intended, not
intending to ask for any
1897 reports till ours were ready.
Then Mr. Lucas read his report on
