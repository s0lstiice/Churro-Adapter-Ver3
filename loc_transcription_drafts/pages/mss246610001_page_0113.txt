Davis said treaty bill be arranged
before August when worst part of

modern fruit as the finding of
seal experts practically sustained
our position. He said however he was
authorized by the President that the
settlement of all disputed matters

taken up in good faith for adjustment.
Finally it was agreed that
each should give to the other a
memorandum of their respective
suggestions.

Wednesday, Foster showed me a draft
of his proposition to Lantier - he offers
a convention to dispose of seal question
- immigration, land fisheries and any
other unsettled question. The two Ents
desire to offer; a modus viv. of one
year prohib. all killing in N. Pac. ocean
& Being Sea & on Pub. islands for one.
year.
10 Am. Saw Lantier and Sir Louis
Davis off at train.
Henry Rud. here - offered to retain
me in matter of assessing extra duty
on dutch sugar bill. of alleged bounty
on exportation given by Netherlands
Govt. I assured him what my services
would be worth to him - he said if the
Amer. sugar refining and other industries
combined in the matter and he intima-
-ted they probably would - that twenty
five thousand dollars a year would
be a reasonable compensation
I told him frankly that I did not
care to accept a retainer in sugar
matters - but that of course I could
always gladly advise him as a friend
without compensation.
