to the State election. Committee will take place this
evening. Said there had been a bitter row
-that Lee refused to pull out for him &
: he should not be a candidate; that he
felt he ought to say to me that he under-
-stood perfectly that it was to be elected
a delegate as arranged by Easton and
that Easton the of me, will address the

honor bound to secure my election. He
