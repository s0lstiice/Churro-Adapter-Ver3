July
22 Received letter from ex-Pres. Cleveland saying
he did not doubt that my failure to fill
a minority report was the most expedient
course to have taken.
23 Nelson writes me sending copy of a letter
to Holmes of the Herald as to his interview
with Judge Parker. Judge Parker absolutely
sound on all issues.
