June 19 Since last entry have been too busy to look up this
entry.
At En. Douglas' suggestion as above stated Hackett
announced himself as a candidate for Chair of State
Corp. Then Crowley sent letters to all of the
State Corps arranging their support for T. Some did
not reply; others, among them Robert Bennett,
wrote they were pledged to Easton. This proves
-while none of us ever doubted that Easton
wanted this for himself. En. D. one day sent
word he wanted to nominate W. M. Flaherty,
and Matthews. He accord. did at my house.
T. suggested nominating Crowley Chair of Exec.
Corp. who. I warmly endorsed. En. D. said he
did not want any direct American. as it
hurt the Party to have them so prominent. I
obliged & called his attention to the fact
that he was elected under Hackett's leader-
-ship as Chair of Exec. Corp. Finally he said
we could do as we pleased, although later he
questioned this.
At the 1st meeting of State Corp. T. was
nominated elected Chair. Easton wanted it
& finally went to En. D. who told him
equally that he wanted T. G. etc. in
absent. control. The Corp. went as you wish
T. I will yield. The En. respected his
