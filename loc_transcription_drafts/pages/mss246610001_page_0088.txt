Hill seemed to fear that Bryan
would be elected; said he would
probably carry the solid South &
extreme West; in that event he would
only hold 1 or 2 middle western
states; he said he could not yet
figure out McKinleys election
with any certainty.

22. Thursday. Spoke at rally Union Hall
Broadway with John F. Connon and
Therman Whipple. Henry W. Lamb
presided. Before rally dinner with Mr.
and Mrs Biscott Hall.
23. Divid at Vendors with Judge Putnam
Turnell in honor of new District Judge
Judge Brown of Providence. Mr. & Mrs
Putnam, Judge & Mrs Brown, Mr. & Mrs
Therman Howe and a thin Chandelier
was present.

24. Spouse at rally at Waltham with John
F. Connon & William Murphy. Silver man
third to break up meeting put me
ten questions in writing which I
answered. Show over 2 hours shut
night at Waltham. Rally a great
succes.

25. Sunday. Divid w. Edw. Alkmin.
took tea w. Moss Williams.
26. Rally Sound money Democrats
at Music Hall - a great success.
Dr Lovett - Mc Connell cand. for Lieut. Gov.
on Sound money ticket - Hulburt. (cand
Attys Gen) Mr. Bowser, N.Y. Signering Bulletin
and I spoke.
27. Short Northampton Man. Divid w.
this Ada Knowlton at Smith College.
Mrs Hubbard, one of the professors
gave a tea for me in afternoon.
About 75 college girls attended
rally in evening. Took this K.
up river rowing. Next morning
attended chapel w. 900 girls.
Have an hours lecture on U.S.
Treasury to Pal. Econ. & History
classes.
