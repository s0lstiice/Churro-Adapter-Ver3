1892
April 13
51 Made speech at Jefferson dinner of Y.M.C.A. Club
of Man. at Amel. House; Rev. Hatcher of Chur.,
Edw. Atkinson, John E. Russell & others present.
(3S.B.20)
52 Nominated Patrick Maguire for delegate to
Nat. Conv. in short speech: he was elected
by acclamation.
14 Withdrew as candidate for President of Y.M.
Club: A.G. Bullock of Worcester, J. Russell
Read of Burlington & Wm N. Olgood of Malden
were nominated besides myself by nom.
party having 25 signatures. It was deemed
best for the Club to elect a man from the
central part of the State and I with-
draw in favor of Bullock; the other
candidates refused to withdraw: Bullock
received the votes of all my friends and
was elected: the vote was Bullock 131-
Olgood 57- Read 45.
My friends told me that of Bullock's 131
votes at least 100 had been pledged to
me & that I should have been elected
by a good plurality. However, I felt that
the good of the Club demanded it. Bullock's
election and was glad to withdraw
