of delegation to State Convention at Boston.
" " was appointed a member of financial comm. of Dem.
State Cent. Comm; was elected a member at

March. 19 Spent whole afternoon with Gov. Russell at the
State House, advising him as to an interview
he was preparing in which he should formally
resign from his candidacy for Presidt in favor
of Cleveland. (For interview see 3 S.B.4)
" 25 with Gov. Russell helping him prepare a
tariff speech for the coming R.D. campaign.
Sent him my Wool Report on Arlington
Mills.
" 26 Brooklyn Chronicle editorial on myself.
(3 S.B.5)

request of Div. Comm of R. I. Took supper at
Nauaugamutt Hotel with Gov. McKinley of

Bryan of Nebraska. - 1500 people.
