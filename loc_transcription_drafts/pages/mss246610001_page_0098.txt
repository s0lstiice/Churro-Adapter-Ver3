13 Friday. En to New York
14 Saturday. Arrive see. 22 before Alty. He
had previously obtained authority to
appoint w. Carlisle nominally for
B. V.M. RR, the Assoc. Bots of Trade, Best
Mechts Assn & Chamber of Commerce.
Left Washington at noon. Nearly
overcome with heat in Washing to.
1000 evening train from 4.7 for Henderson
Harbor Lane Ontario to spend sunday
with Emil Foster and receive final
instructions for my journey. State Dept
gave me a draft for $1500 for my
expenses.
15 Sunday. At Henderson Harbor w.
Ant See. Cudler, visiting Ex Sir
Foster. Met thine Chang You Hon
Shie. ambassador to the Queen's Jubilee
returning from London. He had his
Secretary Mr. Liang with him. Liang
was educated at Phillipps Andover
academy. friend of Mason Martin.
Liang had just been knighted by
Queen Victoria.
16 Left for Watertown and from there
went to Montreal arriving late in
P.M. Found note from Sir William
Van Horne inviting Harriet & me to
197
17 Tuesday. Left for Vancouver with
Harriet. Very comfortable journey
save last day- train 8 hours late.
Chang Hon joined train on way from
Niagara Falls with suite of 26 in special
car. At Winnipeg met Mr. Comer
Presd't Cent. R.R. Georgia & family in
their private car. Lunched with
them & spent most of the time
until Bannff in their car. Met on
train Mrs & Miss Walters of Grand
Rapids Michigan who went to Japan
with us; Mr. & Mrs Cook, manager of
dry docks in Hong Kong, Mr. & Mrs
Ker of Eng. consul service doctors.
Arrived at Elgin House, Elgin
about 9.30 P.M. Saturday. Met thine
Mr. & Mrs Palmer of Albany an artist
whom I met at Mrs Prugh's in
Albany when I gave my seal
lecture there.
Arrived Vancouver Sunday Aug. 22
