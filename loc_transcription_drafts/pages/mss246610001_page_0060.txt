But. Govt notified State Dept that they would
not agree to Regulations for 1895 to carry out Paris
award - except that part relating to the
seeming under seal of the outfit & arm
of Brit. vessels during closed season and in
British Sea. I forwarded a vigorous letter to
Sec. State protesting in this action - I should
think they had implicitly ratified these
regulations in the War Order in Council of
Feb. 2, 1895 enacted after our Regulations were
annululated - Jan 18, 1895. Acting Sec. of State
will write Pannelfote a vigorous letter
based on mine.
