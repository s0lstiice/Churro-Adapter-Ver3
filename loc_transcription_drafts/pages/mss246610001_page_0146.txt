Sept 23 Col. Howes & Dr Coughlin spent night
w. me. Howes said that he knew that
McNary knew that Williams was to
be bought by Gaston on the question
of mentioning the K& C. platform at
least a month before the convention.
He also said Gaston from the first had
been opposed to W. and intended to
fight him for control of the Party.
I told Howes that the Easter leaders
sawed their delegates by representing
that G. was not opposed to W. & that
previous G. voted for Bryan both in
96 & 1900. I showed him a letter from
Mr Logan in N. Adams quoting from
statements of Gaston to that effect,
and I think I satisfied him that
G's supporters had made these spec-
-ulations.
Sept. 26 wrote Gov. Monroe a stiff letter telling
him his statements in the Herald were
not true & offering to call on him
& satisfy him of this fact.
