Devote from Washington to Pittsfield. Took
train at Pittsfield to Albany. Went to
Mr Ricis mother's house -- Mrs Pinguin
went to St Peters Church Albany
towed 3.15. train for Boston
Left for Washington
at Washington
while in Dalton had long talk w. Munay Crane
in presence of Col. Riel. C. said that after his
conversation with me in Washington some
time before the Convention, he told him that
the only hope for Riel was to commence
a vigorous cry of sound money & insist
on "gold" being in platform -- he went down
to Platt & got him to come out vigorously
& he also went to N.Y. Sun & induced it
to follow same course. He also said that
Lodge's praise for insisting upon the
word "gold" in the Rep. Natl. platform
amused him; that Lodge came to him
just before the Convention at St Louis and
stated that the word "gold" must not be
in the platform. Crane said he thought
he told L. that it must be specifically
mentioned & that he would pass the
Man. Comm. against L. if he did
not agree to this; that thencker Lodge
surrounded. Considering the panic Lodge
received for his fight for gold these rumors
of want were most significant.
While in Albany Col. H. at the request
of Gen. Tracy I called w. Col. Riel and
Esquire Chambulain, Shipping Comr. when
Gen. Hill at Wolfetti's west, and had
a long interview. Gen. Tracy told me
Hill was doing good work for the
sound money cause. Hill said he
should not come out for Bryan
but said say nothing -- he said
Denforth Chairman of St. Comm. was in
his control absolutely & that his
announcements that N.Y. would
go for Bryan were merely lunambe.
Hill said Bryan told him that in
his judgment this campaign was a fight
of the West & South vs the East.
Hill said he was working hard
for sound money; that he had
pursued the machine to agree
that the State Comm. should endorse
Bryan -- table a resolution to endorse
the platform and now, a gold
man for Govr. if the Trichild men
would agree that they had
declined.
