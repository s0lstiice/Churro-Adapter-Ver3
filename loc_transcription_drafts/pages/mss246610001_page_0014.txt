(3 S.B. 47)
June 12
Sunday. Had conference at Algonquin Club
with Quincy & Matthews as to money needed
to carry on campaign; we decided that we
should need $50,000 exclusive of what we
"spend for naturalization: this estimate
included $15,000 for city of Boston.
We calculated that if poll tax qualifies
had not been abolished we should have
needed just double = $50,000 more.
We decided that if Nat'l Comm wanted
to make a fight for Mass. we could nat-
-uralize 10 or 15,000 and thus surely carry
Mass. This would cost $2 per head on the
average - some would pay for their own
naturalization

1872
June 15 dinner of Gen. Ruf. Leaure at Amwr. House.
addressed by Brooks Adams, Joseph Quincy
and myself.
(3 S.B. 52)
16 Henry W. Swift called and wanted me
to become Gov. Russell's private Sec'y at
$2500 per year; said Gov. would promote
Roads to some commission (he is present
Secretary). I thought it over but
declined the honor.
Left Saturday morning for 3rd Nat'l Convention
at Chicago; took 10.30 A.M. train on B.Ya.
R.R.: we bought all the train and Road agreed
to run it as a Special. At Toledo the morning
of June 19, the N.Y. Cent. RR added 5 or 6
sleeper cars incommodating us greatly and
spoiling our whole trip: we could sit nothing
to eat and was thoroughly disgusted when
we reached Chicago.
At Chicago; we, Burnett, Matthews, Wheelwright,
Quincy and I went put up at the Chicago
Club and got our morale thus. Killar,
Wheelwright and I roomed together at
the Palmer House, paying the modest
sum of $4 per day each for a room without
board.
