at Marion, Illan from July 26 to

Aug. 30 Henry R. Reed gave me a Birth day dinner
at Algonquin Club. Present, Reed, Sig. Butler
Phenias Price, Henry C. Thacher, Col. Taylor of
Boston Globe, Jm. Jones, Jos. Quincy, Nathan
Matthews, Jos. O'Neil & Henry Collison.
The meeting really was to consider
whom to nominate for Governor. There
was practical unanimity that Williams
should not be nominated if it could
possibly be avoided. They all agreed
me to run but I positively declined.
They finally decided to run Carroll
of Springfield.
Sept 3 I went to Boston & went on board "U.S.
S. New York" and "Minneapolis". Given a
salute of 15 guns by Capt Evans of
"New York".
