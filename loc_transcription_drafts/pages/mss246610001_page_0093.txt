I told Mr. Haz of critical condition of
our seal negotiations & said in my
opinion Sherman Secy of State was
too busy & too old to attend to the matter
vigorously -- that a crisis was at hand
and that Ex-Secy Foster should be
called in and given entire charge of
the subject. Haz agreed with me
that nothing could be hoped for
from Sherman and asked me to
write a letter for the President to
send to Sherman to give the matter
to Foster. I did so & Haz said, me
afterwards the President had signed
it & said he would send it.
The fact that the President would sign
such a letter shows conclusively that
in his opinion Sherman is too old to
be of any use in the Cabinet.

I was now more afficted in my
life. Signed the mail and at 5.10
P.M. signed my name for the last
time as Ant Secretry of the United
States Treasury. This last act was
a refusal to permit the American
Sugar Refinery to change the law
declared in an interview on ground
of a clerical error.

Wednesday morning.
