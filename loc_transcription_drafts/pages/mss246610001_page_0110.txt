Treaty between U.S. Russia & Japan
signed at State Dept today in Diplomatic Room by John W. Foster, David
Stan Jordan and myself in behalf
of the U.S. By Prince Botkin & M. de
Wallant for Russia and by S. Fujita
and K. Mitsukuri for Japan.
Provides for new convention between
U.S. Russia, Japan & St Britain to
consider proper means of preserving
seat herd now threatened with
extermination - for a modus stopping
all killing at sea for one year. The
convention is not to be binding until
ratiified by St Britain.
A secret agreement or note signed by
U.S. delegates was given to Japanese
delegates providing that in case any
compensation were given to St Brit. or
promised to induce her to come into
the convention, equivalent compensation should be given by Japan.
The protocols were signed at same
time as the Treaty and a typed written
summary of all the proceedings was
given each delegate.
