The explanation of this is as follows:-
$31,157,700. of the rent of bonds were taken
abroad. But began to run Feb. 1, pay. Quarterly,
first payt = May 1, being just 1 1/2 (years 4 1/2).
The interest in whole foreign rent = $3115777.00
each quarter = $1246,308.00 annually.
If the bonds taken abroad $16,643,361. were
paid for prior to May 1, the date of first payt of
interest; the quarterly interest on them amounted
to $166433.61.
The delivery of these bonds did not take place
but after May 1 and it was agreed that
the overdue coupons should be taken by us
as cash. When the Syndicate made its
final payt of abt 7 millions, on the bonds
it included therein $166433.61 given as
security that the coupons to that amount
should be delivered to Curtis abroad, the
understanding being that this money should
be returned when the coupons were delivered.
Curtis cabled us that the coupons had
been duly delivered & cancelled (i.e. the
$166433.61). The syndicate, July 8, called
upon Jordan for return of the said money
but J. refused on ground that he had no
authority as he had issued certifics of
deposit in regular form, and the money
had been carried into Treasury.

On inquiring I learned that neither the certifics
for the 7 millions payt which included the $166433.61
- nor the certifics for the $166668.81 (which 125)
had been carried into Treasury, Huntington as a
matter of precaution having delayed this.
I inquired of Darram, chief of Public
Loomys Divn and learned that certifics of
deposit are frequently sold to Sub Marshals
with instructions to cancel them & refund the
money deposited making proper, on the
books. E.g. a merchant intending to import goods
at Phila deposits $100,000 thus in Sub Marshals
pay duties; subject the goods come in at
N.Y. he pays the duties then -- the money
paid at Phila is refunded & certific cancelled.
I suggested that we refund the exact
amount paid as security viz: $166433.61
but Huntington said that a new certific of
deposit would then have to be made out
for the balance of the 7 millions and that
as the first certific. called for Bonds it
would be somewhat difficult to adjust
our books properly.
