78
Feb. 6. Attended public evening reception at
White House - fruit look dinner with
Sec'y Smith. After reception omitted
to collation up stairs with cabinet
and others.
Feb. 11. Went for cruise on Light House Steamer
Maple with Harriet & Jane, Sec'y Van Carlick,
Mr Grisham and Miss Berry & Logan Coubide.
Returned Feb. 18. Went to Point Comfort,
Newport News, Norfolk, Portsmouth and
Yorktown. Had dinner parade at Fort Fisher.
Feb. 18 Dined at Senator Callis. Present Sen. Miles
Gresham, Mr Manning, Mr. Weckrussin (Minister
from Netherlands) Sen. Cyrus Shurvat (Nwada) and
Lieut. Southland.
Feb. 20 Dined at French Ambassador - Mr. Patenotre.
Present Ant. Sie's Uhl & Stiebel (State), Tatino
Japanese Ambassador, Rora (Philippine Islands)
and others.
