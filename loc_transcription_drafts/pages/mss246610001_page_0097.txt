Benedict & myself.
29 Thomas P. Beal gave lunch at Union
Club to Gage - I was present. 3.18 went
back to Marion.
5.30 "Anicida" came over w. Benedict
we all had tea on board - Dr Hand
gave Benedict a chrysot of liberty.
6.30 Yacht steamed for N.Y. w. Sec.
& Mrs Gage.
The weather was very rainy; Mrs S.
had child & was sick abed most
of time. Mrs G. told Harriet that Sec.
Gage had prepared an elaborate
speech - the effort of his life - but
that President McKinley made him cut
out almost all of it for fear of its
effect on Ohio elections. As it was his
speech was hopeful but non-committal.
August
6 In New York. Met Gen. Foster on St Louis.
went to Washington with him.
7 In Washington. Conference with Secy
State. Foster thinks advice for me to go
to Japan as Gen. Govt. are inclined
not to wish w. us but of the Hawaiian
question.
Foster showed me draft of arrangt w.
195
29 Russia in col. he agreed that Russia should
have benefit of all future changes in sailing
regulations etc made by U.S. & N.S. I
objected to this - said all we ought to do
would be to agree to use our best
efforts to obtain for Russia the same
benefit for her ships as we obtained
for ours. I thought a binding agree
might be a break of spirit of Paris
award. It diffused from me & presumably
will disperse this w. President
McKinley when he is to go Monday.
9 Foster writes from Love Champaign
that President desires me to go to
Japan.
10 Wednesday. In Montreal. met w. Sir
William Van Horne as to Sec. 22. Taught Act
Met Sec. Alger (Sec War) there also: he had
been in Canada w. Sir William. They both
advised me to retain Gen. Edmonds & if
he could not appear before Attorney Gen. to
remain Choate, Carter or Carlisle.
11 Boston suspending arrangement. Attorney Gen.
appts Saturday for Hearing. Edmonds
declines remain on ground that he is
counsel for Amer. Shipping associate
Carter in Europe, Choate cannot come.
Finally remain Carlisle
