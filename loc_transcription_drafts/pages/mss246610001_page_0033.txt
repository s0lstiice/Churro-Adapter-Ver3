June 14 went to Alexandria to see old furniture
with Mr Graham, Carlisle & Bissell.
" 16 went to Boston to attend Westford
academy exhibition: returned June 17,
saw Cust. Comte at N.Y. in evening.
" 17 Took Bearfart with Sigs & Mr
Carlisle.
O'Brien Moon, const. of St Louis Rbk. told
me he had heard from authoritative
sources how I was apt'd acting Sec'y. He sd Cleveland id to Civil. at
a Cab. meeting just before they went
to Chicago to open World's fair, that
Mr. Curtis was a discreet man &
he wanted to apt him Acting Sec'y:-
Civil. said to Cl. that Mr. Hamlin was
also a discreet man and a much
better Demoerat than Curtis yet was
his personal wish that Hamlin should
be made Acting Sec'y whenever he
could not act. (As Moon is a very
intimateite terms w. Sec'y Carlisle this inference must have come from himself.)
June 24 went to Chapel Point on Potomac. Met with
Dist. Mar. Div. Club: deliber'd addition together
with Conf. Bynum (stud)- Ex Gen. Black (Pa) Vet. Assn.
(S.C. Bk. p.)

July 3
1863
Sec'y Carlisle & wife and Logan Carlisle
have tomorrow for World's fair to be
gone two weeks. I am to send as
acting Secretary till their return.
" 21
Sec. Carlisle returned to Washington. I had
prepared in his absent statistics on
the Silver question in response to a
letter from Sig. Lamont from Gray Gables.
Carlisle suggested that as I was going
East for a few days to visit my family
that I stop at Gray Gables & give the
statistics to the President. I telegraphed
light house tender to meet me at
New Bedford.
22 Left for N.Y. Had conference with
Macfarlane of tariff investigating Comm't.
took short line for Boston.
23 5 A.M. Left train at Providence
went to New Bedford. Boat did not
meet me. Took cab. & drove to Marion
-look dinner w. Mid.-ny brother - then
drive to Gray Gables. Have Presd Cleveland
the statement found Lamont & Bys Bryant
thru.
President appeared not well at all:
had his mouth evidently packed
