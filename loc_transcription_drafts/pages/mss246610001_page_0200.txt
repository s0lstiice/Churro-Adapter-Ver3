July 11
In New York to meet J.B. Thayer V. President P&RR who
wrote saying he wanted to see me. Spent one after-
noon last month at his house in Hartford; he
thim it when the vote was become low he should
ask the other RRs to give me embroiling counsel
to represent the principal RRs in N.Y. before the
but. Com. Com. and that he should support my name.
To day he told me he had consulted w. the
Boil, Mr. Randolph V. Presdt and w. Mr. Caldwel
of the D.L.D.W. and w. Mr. Manny, Em. Commel of
the P&RR and that they all agreed it was
the man for the place. He said his idea was
to have the P&RR. Phil & Reading. United RRs N.Y.
& Central of N.J., D. L.D.W. N.Y. Cent. together
employing me. Said he thought it would
have to live on work in N.Y. - probably the latter.
I asked him what compensation would
be paid at least $25000 per annum and
very easily must. I told him it could
hardly afford to give up the Boil. and for
$25000 move to N.Y. where my expenses could
be so much greater. He said that would
be easily arranged so that I should be
satisfied. I sold 30000 wild hogs. In
the limit I did turn up I had to go to
N.Y. to live. I then asked if it could not be
