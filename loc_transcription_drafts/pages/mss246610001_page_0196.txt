27 Have had Mr Cleveland breakfast at Union
Club. Then took them to B&O R.R., called on
Rudelt Institute, & took them to train. Had good
late w. Mr. C. told him about Eastern attitude
on me cause, bee. I did not file seriously
plans for gold in Nat. Currency last year. C.
said it was outrageous - he advised me not
to worry - said my time was not yet come.
That he had every confidence it would come
but to go so slowly; &d Eastern this following
the intending to ignore me, went really
unwittingly doing me a great service, in
keeping me back a while from political
activity.
He spoke of Nat. matters - said that he
believed that Roosevelt would be found
out by Annex. people and was in danger
of leaving office the most discredited
of Amer. Presidents.
