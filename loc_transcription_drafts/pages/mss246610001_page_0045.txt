90
May 22 Sicy Lament sent for me: & Gen Gray
told him the Gen. Court had heard rumors
that the Whig's Club of Phila had raised
the money w/ Mr. Burtz had offered
Gen Cleiby & Hunter to vote vs Tammy Bill.
Asked if I did investigate it. I said
Sp/sa Hapton of Phila might learn
about it & that I would telegraph
him to come on & ask him to investigate
it
May 24 Logan Calhoun told me that Sacy C.
let me know that the President, when asked
to appoint Gen. Gray on Supreme Bench
after Humbles & Pickham had been
rejected, said that "he would be
damned if Gray would sit on the
Supreme Bench while he was President."
I let support his feeling on the matter
was caused by the weak, timid course
Gen. Gray pursued in the Senate while
the Bill to Repeal Sherman Silver Puchion
law was pending; Gen. G. was the first
of the Adams friends to give in &
sign proposed compromise: if it had
not been for Presidents courage the Bill
might never have become law.

learn that he is drunk. When Sec. C.
and I went off on light house boat
last Sacy Wink was down about for
2 or 3 days. The Sacy told him if it
occurred again he should resignate

ways & Means Comm. Present: -- The Vice Presd.

Turney, Cliff Bueckensmidt - Em. Dively. A. Neil

to concert at Metzger Hall to hear Mr.

31 Geo. Hens. & Jane sailed on steamer & Miners
