the interest of Hill for the Presidency
as against Cleveland and that Crisis as
Sprague was glad to do anything that
Hill told him to do.
Also had interviews with Haster (Ohio),
Wilson (W.Va), Spraker Crisp, Senator Gorman
and others:- had long talk with Bland.
From what I saw I was convinced that
the Hill men did not wish to have the
silver question settled directly in the
House; they wished to drag the question
along, keeping it before the public as a
means of disfracing Cleveland; they did
not care to have the Bill actually pass
the House but they desired to have it
come as near that result as possible.
