Thursday, left for Boston to give
speech late in Alaska at University Club.
Friday. In Boston. went to Quincy to meet
him. In evening went out to Robert Morris
& spent night going to Hay. Elu Club concert.
Called on Patrick Moynir. He said he would
go as a delegate to the Nat'l Convention &
that he was friendly to Carlisle.
10. Coming. Smere. late in Alaska at University
Club. Large attendance. Wilson Warren
gave me a dinner before the lecture.
Present:--Francis Ashelton, Bridt J. M. Rich.
Club:--Chas P. Harraghty--Geo. Scheffy--Ex. Gov
Russell--Charles Jefferson. Mayor Adams
of Quincy. Commander Grover U.S.N.--
in Boston. went to church of our Saviour.
Lunched with C.S. Greenough.
11. At funeral of Rev. Gumbalge at Lowell.
Sunning. gave dinner to Quincy, Matthews,
& Judge Corcoran. Talked over political
field. All agreed that Russell should
not be endorsed for the Presidency as
Whig was of far greater national import.
Quincy thought: as did also O'Neil
that Whigs friends would be in a majority
