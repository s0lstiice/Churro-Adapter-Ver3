July 24
My interview announcing candidacy for Gov
published in all the papers -- I confined
it to State issues. Originally came out also
on Natl issues but Char. Warren advised
me to omit Natl issues as Bryan was
to speak July 24 at Nantucket & he might
think I was trying to force his hand
and be offended.

1902
July 24
Meeting N.C. Dem. League at Nant. Beach. Bryan,
Shepard, & Sen. Cannon stood. Mr. Clancy had
left me he would also speak but he was so
indignant over Bryans attacks on Cleveland
in the Commoner that he declined to go.
Some friends thought no Cleveland man
should go but I thought it all the more
reason for the attendance of Gold Democrats.
Accordingly I went over to see Ex. Presd's
Cleveland ad Gray Eables and told him it
intended to go as a Gold Dem: though if Bryan
attacked him I shed at once with vehly
and vigorously defend his Adm't whether
the result to my candidacy. He seemed
perfectly willing to have me go but feared
it might injure my prospects. I said I
was willing to leave the risk. Mr. C. said,
speaking of the Tilden Club dinner, that
it was against his own judgment that he
attended but that he was absolutely
forced to go by the Club officials.
July 18 Edw. M. Shepard spent the day
with me at Mattapoisett -- I urged him
to attend the Natl. dinner and he finally
became satisfied that it was the
wisest course to pursue.
