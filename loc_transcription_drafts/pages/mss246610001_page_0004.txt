1892
Jan. 1 I went to Washington to interview Speaker
Crisp: he assured me Taits Reform had
ried no backward step by his election. He
aided me whether Stevens or Andrews assist
always & Illinois Comts would give most
satisfactn in Illan; 2nd Andrews allthis
is absent would cause us dissat-
-isfaction.
Took lunch w. Stevens, Walter Trunk, and Mr.
Springer (afterwards Chairman Ways & Means
Comts). I made Stevens to interviewed on
Taits question: he had had an interview
prepared by Mr. Conant, of Springfield Rebut-
ted; I praised it, adding much and
having out much: he afterwards gave it
to Priv. (S.B.2-65)
went back to N.Y. to see Ex-Presdt Cleveland;
found note from him saying he couldn't
meet me in N.Y. until the next week;
I couldn't wait to meet him and so
wrote him. My object was to invite
him to come to J.M. Dem. Club dinner
next April 20.
Dec. 21. Spoke at annual dinner J.M. Dem. Club
of Mans. at Amer. House. (S.B.2-67)
23rd Held interview with President Walker of
Inst. Philology and J.H. O'Neil on plan
of holding Centm. Military conference.
Showed at dinner of Mans. Reform Club;
James Mans was invited to explain the
principles of the new Columbian Party and
I was one of several who opposed the
merit of such a new party. (S.B.2-59)
