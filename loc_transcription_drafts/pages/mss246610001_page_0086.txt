Sept. 19
Invited by Att. Gen Harmon to go to Mt Vernon
with Lord Russell (the Lord Chief Justice of
St Britain). Went on special election car.
Lady Russell, Min Russell, Mr. Fox well w.
Ed Rip party. Also Sirg of Interior Francis,
Sol. Gen. Conrad, Ex. Ant Atty G. Maury, Judge
Harlan (U.S. Sup Ct) Miss Harlan, Gen. Emily.
On arriving Mt Vernon Lord Russell planted
3 trees, an English oak, English laurel and
an Eng. walnut. Came back by boat.
In evening Calldon Calhoun the Atty for
But. legislature -- the only But. Representative
in the City -- gave a dinner at Country Club.
He invited all of the party except
myself. As I was Acting Secy of the
