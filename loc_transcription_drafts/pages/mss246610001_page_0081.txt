Jun. 9
Govt. Cooloeat resigned Union Bill
Had long interview w. Sec. Olney as to the
Venezuela boundary dispute. He said he had
absolutely no confidence in Ambassador
Bayard. that he had practically done nothing
on the Govt. while in England -- that he had
received advice from people in whom
he had absolute confidence that the Guyana
people looked on Bayard as an miserable
old bore w. out any influence.
