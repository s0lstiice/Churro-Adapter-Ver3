in dressing rooms as instructed & in clubs

was not segregate but merely a generalizing general
out of the class of work wh. absolutly

I told Ralph to leave his fact away from
segregation in work rooms, but to proceed
very slowly & cautiously, using tact and good
judgement striking no muscle and
fair treatment on the goal. Strongly advised
no public interviews. Suffered in him
that what I wanted was to bilbo the
colored employes and bring about good
feeling & harmony, without any flaming
pronouncements which would do them
more harm than good.
