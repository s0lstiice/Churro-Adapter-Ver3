5.45 P.M. Sen. Brandtys has just telephoned
that I was just confirmed by the
Senate without a dissenting vote.
A minute later Sen. Haller telephoned to
same effect, and said Senate passed
a vote to notify the President of the
confirmation.
I at once went home & called up
Boston at 6.45 told her & then sent a
written to Sec. Medders on the Amundsen
in Long Island Sound.
