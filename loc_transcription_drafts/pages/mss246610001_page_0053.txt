Dec. 6
Called on Holmes post of Bos. Herald.
complained again of Boekens-H. N. He
wanted to treat Carlisle fairly - I told
him I had written an editorial stating
both sides of currency question fairly
& in today Sec. Carlisle fairly - At his
request I showed it to him & he said
he would publish it which he did
this night (Friday Dec 6) morning
