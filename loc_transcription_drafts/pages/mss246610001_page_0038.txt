76
19 invited & went to theater with Miss Sanders
cousin of Secy Lamonot, Mrs Cleveland &
Min Huddleston cousin of Mr Cleveland.
25 attended Callled on Mrs Cleveland 5 P.M.
She received me in parlor & entertained with
the President. Francis Cleveland & Evans
the actor were there with their wives.
Saw Esther & Ruth, the children; also the
President's sister Min Rose Cleveland.
Evening.
Attended Presidents reception to Justice
& Congress: invited by Mrs Cleveland
to go behind line.
29 Dined at Mr. Hubert's, Secy of Navy; then
were present Miss Hubert, Secy H., Tanner, the
Austrian Minister, Baron Ketter, Secy of
Yemen. Legation - Miss Pannerfoote daughter
of Cus. Ambassador, Patent Office and others.
31 Miss Cleveland sister of President C.
wrote asking me to go with her to
the Capitol to hear Taft's debate, we
went down w. Mrs Manning, widow of
Ex-Sen. Hannifis, but stayed only a
short time. Miss C. T. De walked home
together.
77
194 went to Capitol again w. Miss Cleveland.
Third final debate, Reed, Crist & Wilson.
A memorable seat. Mrs Manning & her
wife Miss Fryer, Mrs Harriet Lane Johnston
(vice of Presd. Buchanan), her niece Miss
Kennedy went with us. We all sat in
the President's seat in the Gallery. Miss
Cleveland, Mrs Johnston & I walked
home from the Capitol after the
debate.
2 Invited by President & Mrs Cleveland to dine at
White House: attended there: There were
present, the President, Mrs Cleveland, Mr. &
Mrs Gilder, Miss Cleveland, Mr. & Mrs Thacher
Roosevelt (Civil Service Comm.), Mr. Proctor
(Civil Serv. Comm.), Mrs Daniel Manning and
myself.
5 Invited w. Harriet to attend cabinet dinner
given by Secy Morton to Presd. & Mrs
Cleveland; we both attended. There were
present, - Presd. & Mrs Cleveland. Miss Cleveland
Ex Secy Bristow, the Cabinet Secretaries (except
Post G. Rawl) & the sons & their wives of
Secy Morton.
