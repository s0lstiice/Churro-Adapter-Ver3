1906
May 11 Sunday. Chinese Minister arrived 7 am for
Washington. Went to Somerset then took Crawford
with me. Then we went to term. Station to meet
Chinese Imperial Com. In particulars on their
visit see secret Bk.
Sunday evening joint dinner for Corn. & Chin.
Minister. Present. Comr Tai, Comr Tuan, En Chintung
Worthern Guild, Mr Thos Richard Clancy, Mrs
Phillipps, Mrs John L. Gardner, Mrs Roger Wolleott,
Mrs Edw. Hamlin, Mrs Geo. Hamlin, Mr Francis
Bartlett, Mr Tylt.
May
Robt Crowley came to office & wanted me
to consult Dr Coughlin to see if I would
not agree to come out for Jno B. Moran
for Dem. cand. for Govt & receive in return
a pledge from his friends to support me
for Govt in future. I gave him no
encouragement -- so I was decidedly
adverse to any such action.
Dr Coughlin called at office -- I told
him what Crowley said. He agreed w.
me that I should make no such
alliance. Advised me not to run for
Govt. in New York. seat. Said Greens old
best man under me was willing to spend
25,000. Said it could beat any one else in
district easily.

May 25
While Cornell lunched w. me at Union Club.
Said Me Swenley told him I was going to
come out for Elloran. I told him just
what had happened. Told him I still
be glad to have run for Cong. in 41 district
but would enter into no contest for it.
May 31 Short at Dark Mohawk N.Y. at Pearl
Conference. Better wind with me.
