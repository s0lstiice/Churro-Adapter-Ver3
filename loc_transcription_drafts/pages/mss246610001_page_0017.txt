Linney asked me to ask him if he
would receive Gen. Benjamin Butler at
my house if he called: Gen. B. had
intimated that he would live to call.
Me. C. was rather anxious to this: he
did not leave B's public record &
thought it unfair publicly to meet
him at his (C's) solicitation: while he
would meet B. of course if he came
to him yet in no way would he commit
me that a visit from him
would be pleasing to him; he considered
him a dishonorable man and he could
not invite him to come to me. I am
sure if a refusal would lose him
many votes.
He walked with me from his house to
the new station just outside his grounds
and waited 20 minutes until the
train came.
