Mr Carlisle died suddenly at Baltimore & I
went to funeral. Mr Miles Cleveland went
there, Henry Waterson it etc.
Henry M. Whiting telegraphed me that Gov. Douglas
would like to see me. Met him at Townsville
Boston. He said he wanted me to run on
Gov and that this would be a public
endiment for me that would save Easton
the State Machine; said it ought to alter-
—tive trade schools etc; that it would
cost $15000 and that he would pay
the entire expense if I would run.
I told him that I could not enter
into any contest for the nomination, that
if the Party with reasonable unanimity
should ask me to run that would be
another matter, but at still do nothing
to bring this about, he said I knew
the machine would not nominate me but
I told him I would not run as
a money campaign & the machine
could have to gain the nomination
by any means except money.
He asked me subscription from any person on

corporation which looked thereby to secure any

I knew it would not be. Also told him that
it could not accept any large subject
from anyone as it could put me under
obligations wh. I could decline to assume;
that I could accept a liberal contribution
from him, however, as I knew his motive
would be entirely selfish. I further said
I hoped the delegates would be elected
before any candidates went on the field
as they could be more liable to consult the
best interests of the Party.
Mr. Collyer before this sent for me &
sunged me to run; said he old being out
a strong public sentiment for me but I
said same to him as to Douglas.
Mr. Ellings also met me in town & said I
was the man for the situation and would
certainly be nominated for me not for "that
darned Gaston."
Bartlett & Whiting nominated.
I was appointed on Com. of Resolutions and helped
draft platform. Affixed a resolution in death of
Collins.
