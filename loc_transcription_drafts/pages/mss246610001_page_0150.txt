Remained in London two weeks. First
went to Buckleys Hotel - paid 2 1/2 $
per day for sitting room and small
bed room. Room was too high up
and too small. We could only rent
the rooms 3 days as it being in the
steam they were taken. Then we
moved to Bucklands, Brook St
where we had to pay 3 quinies
for sitting room, bed room & private
bath. 2nd week we took a cheaper
suite for $2-8sh. (Hotel at Evid
best was the Coburg where the
rooms were much cheaper than at
Bucklands & very mil.)
Hamilton Robt & family came to
Bucklands before we left.

May 12th. Miss Chambolain, Lord Peel
