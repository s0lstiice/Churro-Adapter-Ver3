5
1891
We left Col. Edgely at 2 a.m. Sunday morn.
night; I took 3 a.m. train for Boston.
Gov. Russell boarded Boston train for New
York to go to see Mr. Cleveland (Ex-President);
we waded all reports and the Governor's
trip to N.Y. was never known.
