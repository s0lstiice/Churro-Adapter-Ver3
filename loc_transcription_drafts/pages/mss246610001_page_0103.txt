9. Thursday.
Baron Kikkawa called. Also Fujita.
Spent day driving with Kikkawa in
Hypna Park etc. Mr. & Mrs Buck
called.
10 Friday.
Called w. Mr Buck on Marquis Saigo,
Minister of Marine. Also left cards on
other cabinet officers and on following
ministers: Italy, Belgium, Germany,
Gr. Britain. Also on Count Kobayama
Min. Home affairs. Prof Mitamumi called.
11 Saturday.
In Yokohama shopping. In evening
dined w. Admiral McNair on Olympia.
Havit also then and Mr. & Mrs. Buck,
Cons. Gen. Lowry & others.
12 Sunday.
Went to Kamakura with Mr & Mrs Lisboa
and family the Brazilian Minister.
While there Mrs Lisboa said I reminded
her of an American who was once
her sweetheart - a Mr. Easter Coming
Burgh of Albany. said his voice &
face were just like man. Later I learned
from Mrs Brugh of Albany that he was a
son of her late husband by his first wife -
he died some years ago.

12 Thursday.
Visited several temples & took long ride
in Yndieshion - visited the famous
statue of Buddha. Spent night at
Yokohama.
13 Monday.
Called on Mr Payne agent C. P. RR
Went to Tokio at noon. called on Baron
Kikkawa in afternoon - met his wife
and family.
4 P.M. Barnd business. Kikkawa called
also Mr. Edwin Haweson Minister and Mr.
Devinson.
Went with Havitt and Baron & Baroness
Kikkawa to the Maple Club where we
were given a Japanese dinner. Fujita &
his wife, Mitamumi wife and others were
present. Between each course Eisho girl
sang and dances performed. The menu
consisted of the liquor called sake;
also had soup, raw fish, clams, lobster
cooked fish, oval, fowl, sweetmeats &
culinoral tea.
