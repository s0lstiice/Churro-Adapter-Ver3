Sept 4 Shore Ellsworth Manit
Bar Harbor, Maine.
10 Div. caucuses. Eastern wins. Sec. S. Boone.
16 Divine of State Cornet Friends at Revue
House in afternoon; in evening meetings of
Result Comt and general gathering of
delegates. I had head quarters in a suite
of hotel.
Although the convention showed that Eastern
had badly defeated me -- the best count
I made giving me not more than
500 delegates. yet Dr. Coughlin and many
of my other friends advised me to
go to a vote in the Convention as a
protest in Eastern's methods. Although
strongly urged my withdrawal, Mr.
Griffin editor of the Shipping. Republican
advised me not to withdraw and
at 1.45 in the morning in my room
at the Revue House I made up my
mind to stay in.
The next morning I look rooms at
Parker House. About 10 am Mr
Griffin & Dr Coughlin were in my room.
I had that morning for the first
time seen Williams' & Quincy's platform
and the clause in his platform
late Thursday night Williams told
me the general nature of
the platform of the
East Natural Cornet. I saw nothing
objectionable in its form and asked if
anything in the preceding paragraphs
which it purported to induce referred
to fine silver or financial questions. He
said absolutely nothing.
The next morning -- I read the
whole platform in the newspapers.
While I did not like its form and
questioned its reference to the effect
of Capitalism on the "pens, profit and
collars" also its crude way of
referring to East by Eugine, yet the
principles were not objectionable.
