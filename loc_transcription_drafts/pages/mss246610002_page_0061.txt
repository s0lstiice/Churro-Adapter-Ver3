He spoke of the signs in toilet rooms as

regulation. I assured him of any sign
went to be seen in new building & he said

I also said should depend on him to

satisfaction, I think, that I want to do

Dan'l Country called. Said his relative was to marry
niece of Madame Ursicill the dressmaker who was
under indictment for smuggling in Boston; that
her attorney Mr. Shutterman was in town, w. letter to
attorney. I am sure attorney would advise
letter. I asked him to send me a letter to
judge of courts and leave him case in
place of judge's instruction; that no court could
send him to jail if that law friends could
raise 10,000 to buy fine wh. was more than
court could in town. He asked as to Treasurer
and I said any proportion for settlement
will do, he sent to Treasury referred to
the judge; that they probably could not return
to any disposition of case other than what
it would order. He said he was in trouble
w. family attorney for other debts & he knew he
could arrange to have other debts silent
