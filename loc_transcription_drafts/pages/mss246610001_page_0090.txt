President told me of his quarrel with Henry
Mathison (see entry 31). Said W. had written
him a very sarcastic letter stating that he had
committed his tariff message of 1887 by
pract and making other very sarcastic
allusions to the Presidents alleged course
in dictating the tariff plans of the Party.
The President said he wrote a sharp letter
in reply stating among other things that
if W. knew the tariff message by heart
he certainly gave no evidence of it. Further
said that he had never seen or had any
thing to do with any of the tariff plans
except that of 1888. This one had been
brought to him & he made some suggestions. The convention adopted it as he
left it except that it added an endorsement of his message of 1887 -- a change
which had occurred to him but which
this moderator, he had not put in.
He said W. wants him in reply that all was
now between them.
