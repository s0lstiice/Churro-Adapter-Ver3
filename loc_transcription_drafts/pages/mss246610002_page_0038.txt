Tuesday.
Arnold called & gave me return to show John Lowman
found his fine goods were being confiscated as service
in violation of law. He had appointed w. Alty Em. at
2 P.M. I said I should not attend unless the correct
act. but I did not hear from him. He said he
found no doubt but that JW. knew of this illegal
practice.
