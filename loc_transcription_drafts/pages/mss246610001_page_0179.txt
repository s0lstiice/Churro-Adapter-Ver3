At about 9.30 I went out to the World's
Fair & rode around the grounds in the
light. 11.30 I then came home. This was
all it said of the Fair.
At 2 P.M. when convention came together
Hastin told me that Parson had sent a
telegram. Douglas & Thayer said it had
done just right on not failing a minor
report.
Finally the convention adjourned to 8 P.M. to
decide as to the telegram. I made up my
mind that I ought to sleep in

failing minor report.
