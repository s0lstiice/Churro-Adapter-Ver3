May 11 Sec. Carlisle and I today decided
definitely not to pay the claim of
Hutchinson Co for refunds on hot
linenings amounting to $195,000 in
four suits alone. A warrant had
been made out under last admin-
istration was ready to be made when
on Dec. 2 1893 Presd' Harrison ordered
that it be not paid. The claim con-
sisted largely of cotton back satin
hiss goods on which the Ent got a
verdict in Meyer vs Cadwallader.
Under the procedure adopted in
this case no trial by jury was held
but the questions of fact were
refused to a Court of 3 of the
U.S. Gen. Cops for determination. The
claim about refund was ordered.
We considered that the mode adopt-
ed was illegal - that the Court did
not have all the evidence before
them - that the result was certain
to Philadelphia when practically
similar goods were found by jury
not to be cotton but Co. or chiefly
used as such (this verdict was
later overturned by Court but not
89
on the merits) and in addition we believed
that all of the protests were invalid
under R.S. 2931 as affected by 3011
(my legal point on which I submitted
a brief to Att'y Gen.).
In view of the bad effect such a
settlement would have upon
other pending cases (1690 in N.Y.
alone involving total claims of
over 55 millions of dollars), and
of the verdict in Philadelphia case and
the doubt as to legality of protests
we decided that the case must
be tried by a Jury.
May 8
James Carter of N.Y. argued the real
contract claim of N.S. vs N.Amer. Com.
Co. today. Secy Carlisle decided to
refer the matter to Att'y Gen. for
action against the Company, thus
maintaining my ruling that Secy Fortner
settlement was illegal.
May 12
N.Amer. Com. Co. formally notified of
above conclusion & letter sent to
Att'y General.
