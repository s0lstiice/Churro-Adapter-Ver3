1904
Friday. Am. Junney telephoned me
that he was authorized by Col.
Easton to ask me to vote for Me S.
I had him advised Q. to tell P.
that if as candidate he wanted MeS
elected he shild come out and say
so, and this preclusive response
by Q. made me rather angry.
I at once said to Junney that I
could not accede to Col. P request
that, as at present advised, I
did not intend to be dragged
into any personal controversy
with Me N. & Me Sec. & G. and shild
prefer to resign. (I had told MeN.
last night just the same and said
I would resign from the Sub Comm’ce).
Q. said he thought it ought to
remain on the Sub Comm’ce to arrange
the details of the Convention and then
later I old resign if I saw fit.
I finally determined not to resign
at least for the present.
Exec. Comm’ce meeting 2 P.M. worried
3 hours on details.
Junney also said to me that ColP
had had a conference w. McNary
313
and that MeN. had agreed to allow
the MeS. matter to be dropped for
the present and MeN. confirmed
this to me later saying that as long
as it was deferred MeS. was still
with us.
After the meeting Robt Crowley said
he thought there could be a fight to
the finish on the MeS. matter and
that I ought to vote for MeS.
The Council was with me and he
thought that my refusal to comply
with his request as expressed to Q. was
enough of a stand to take at himself
at least.
Feb 23 Even Smoove later to Jackson Club led to
Charleston, in Alaska. About 500 hundred.
made an honorary member of Club.
Mar.
16 Meeting Exec. Council & State Council
Exec. Comm’c. Junney told me not to interfere
between McNary & Easton – that neither
party wanted it & that McNary w. in a
few days have openly declared that I
had no influence w. him whatsoever.
