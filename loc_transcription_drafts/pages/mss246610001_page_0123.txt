August 25 Spoke at dinner Old Colony Club at Brunswick
Harbor House, Portsmouth.
Sept. Secretary Eagle of U.S. Treasury writes that Presid.
McKinley has offered a place on Centr. Railroad
Court to Ex. Presd. Cleveland and asks me to
use my best influence to induce him to
accept. I wrote Mr. Cleveland and received
in reply a long letter giving his reasons for
declining and also giving carefully over the
political situation stating his difficulty in
choosing between Bryanism & McKinleyism.
Sept. 15 Gave a lunch to Sec. Eagle at Union Club.
Present: Mr Long, Secy of Navy, Call. of Nat. Rev.
Bill, C.Jackson, T.P. Bcal. Lucius Tuttle, &
Mr Curtis Vice Presd. N.Y. N.H.&H.R.R.
Oct 26 5.23 Am. Batte has daughter born. Weighted 7 5/16 lbs.
Nov 6 Presidential election. I voted for the State ticket
the regular Democratic ticket. For Congressman
I voted for Mc Call. For President
election I voted only for Edward Waldo
Emerson, the Elector & the only Elector on the
National ticket.
