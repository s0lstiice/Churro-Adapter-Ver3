The Presd t & Cabinet left for N.Y. today: Presd t
C. designated me as acting Sec'y of Treasury
whenever Sec. Carlisle was absent - a
general designation indefinite as to
time. My instructions were to buy
gold for all notes. Treasury as well as
Greenbacks.
Sec. Carl. was very much pleased at the
action of the Boston Banks; he told
me he never had contemplated returning
to redeem the Treasury notes in gold;
65
that he was of course unwilling to issue
bonds save as a last resort; that the
only debt of opinion bit. him & Presd t was

first wished an immediate issue while
Carl. objected to it; that the Presd t had
perfectly admitted the Carl. was right in
the matter. Carl. was very bitter
in his feeling towards the N.Y. Banks
for endeavors to leave advantage of
the financial. fleeing for their own selfish
purposes.
J. D. W. Warner told me Carl. had
intended to redeem bills, notes in silver
and N.Y. Com. Bulletin & world had
agreed to sustain him but he at the
last moment gave in to Presd t C.
