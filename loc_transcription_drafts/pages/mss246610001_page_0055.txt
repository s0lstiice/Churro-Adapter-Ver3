me to prepare for him a letter to Sir

suggested advisability of temporary
return to Madras revividi of 1892-93
shutting up Bering Sea but leaving
N. Pacific open. On reflection I have
decided that Mad. Vic. would not afford
relief desired as this Br. Sea was closed
yet the rains could chase the mud
during May June & July in N. Pacific
(closed under award) and would thus
probably kill almost as many as
now. I accordingly requested a request
to be transmitted to Japan, Russia
& England for a joint commission to
investigate Alaska scaling and pending
this inquiry for a modest revividi
extending present regulations across the
N. Pacific to Japan and in addition
total cessation of killing in Bering Sea.
