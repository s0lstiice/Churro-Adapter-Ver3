7th
Sat.
Some days ago Mayor Curley of Boston wrote me
stating that negro delegates had executed on him
he hoped segregation in Mass. Debt would
crack.
I sent our Thunberg & he said there was
segregation today in Mass. Debt, especially in
Dist. Rev. Bureau & in Auditors of P.O. and also in
Sum. of Eng. Printing -- in lunatic room; that several
lectures were provided in Mass. Elders etc. He also
said Ass. Sec. Williams sent our club clerk
Williams & ordered him to segregate all
negroes; that he also sent our club officers
devote etc under him & confidentially told them
to segregate; that he certainly send segregation
did not go nearly far enough.
The gave me full letter to Bill H. Julliett
whos quoted letter from club Bur. Eng. & Printing
95
admitting segregation in lunch room; the particular
case embodied of by his H. Julliett was the removal
of Roserbad C. Attorney for ministeriality Aug. 20
1913 by Ant Sec. Williams. Wanted of this record
favored to disclose her removal bill, & color.
Monday
Mr. Solomon told me that Mr Gantt, Ch. Clerk of
Courts of Dist Rev. had just told him that Mr.
Williams, as one of his last acts as acting Sec.
directed him to remove all colored messengers
now having desks in the room of the Dist
Rev. service into the corridors and keep them
there. Gantt said there had been no complaint
whatever about them. Solomon said he told
Gantt to forget the order and I told Solomon
that was right and that no such order
should be issued or carried out which it
was acting Sec.
