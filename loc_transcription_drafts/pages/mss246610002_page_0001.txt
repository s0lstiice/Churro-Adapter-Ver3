1913.
Mar 11, Tuesday. Rev. Fox offered me place on Navy, Water and
Severalty Board. At first I declined it but he begged
me to take it as a duty to the state. said the President
should offer me a permanent position and that there
was not to stand in the way in slightest degree if
I would only accept it. He said he would gladly
endorse me for Collar or even an Ambassadorship.
Mar 12
1913
Rev Fox called me up at Tavern Club in the afternoon
said Council was in session and begged me to
allow him to send in my name. Finally I said
I would accept and that P.M. he appointed me.
Mar 15 Sat.
Dr Loughtlin came to my house; must pleased at
appointment. Very bitter agent Easter; said he had
circulated a petition over the State for appointment
as Sec. of Navy; St John Cummings told him he had
accepted it; as he had talked w. certain influential
people in Wash. & that the board had decided
Easter would always oppose me and that the
old time Radicals were now very friendly. said he
had an appointment w. Rev. had Williams telegraphed
me telephone; said no one could obtain any confirmation
was very angry w. Rev Fox.
Some day lunched at Union Club with Em Schatz
who had w. him Gen. Wilson, Mr Brandtis & all.
Brandtis was very friendly and cordial; doubtless
he had read my interview in B. Post ( 26th b. )
promising him for cabinet position. I was much pleased
at my colleagues to accept position in Navy, Water
Board, that it was a splendid thing to give me.
