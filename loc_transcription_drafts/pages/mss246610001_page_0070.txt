Nov. 25 Called on Mrs Joseph Peabody. She assured
me if I would serve as Executor and
Trustee under her will. She reminded
me that she assured me once before
but that it suggested Mr. Robert
More insisted & that she followed
my advice. She said that Mr. M.
was much older than she & that
she had made up her mind that
she must have a younger man. I
told her that if she was firm in her
determination to have a younger man
and asked me to act that it would
do so. She said she had more confidence in me than in any living man
and earnestly desired me to serve.
I accordingly consented & she said
she would write me & ask me formally.
She also said she was about

Dr. 17 President sends message to Congress asking
for appropriation for a Commission to be
appointed by him to ascertain true
boundary line between British Guiana
and Venezuela. Also enclosed correspondence
bet. Sec. Clancy & Salisbury on the subject.
Great excitement in Washington and in
country generally. Public sentiment of
the independent element in U.S. & New
England decidedly against Presidents
course; on the other hand the rank
and file of the people seem to be with
the President.
From letters to Sec. Carlisle I gather that
he was opposed to the message as likely
to affect our finances disastrously, although
he said nothing positively from what
Sec. Morton has said, or distinctly speaking
from what he has not said, I gather
that he entertains this same feeling.
