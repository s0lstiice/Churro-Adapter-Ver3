Sept 13
Sid. Bos. Sund. Elks forming presentate Albany
name for Brideway in coming State Convention.
Up to a mo. ago Easter & his crowd were
booming Albany. a prize in Herald evidently
inspired by McSweeny said that the William
Hauler faction were opposed to endorsing
Albany. To settle this I give int. to Elks
as did also Dr Coughlin. Then the machine
turned around and began to throw out
that while they could not present Albany
name yet they did not mean that
they could not endorse him at some
future time etc etc.
Just at this time Griffin of Albany, Rep.
wrote saying he intended to become
letters for Albany endorsement and to
publish them. I advised him to go ahead
and give him out myself.
I have fulfilled every wish possible to
299
sean the machine, Easter etc. Sept. 21 I
called Bennett on teleph. and told him it
would be a fatal mistake not to endorse
Albany and that it must be done. He
said he agreed w. me and had same
opinion as when he took breakfast w. me
at Union Club - a month ago, etc. that it
should be done. He further said that McNary
was opposing it but that it could
save his assurance that he should be
endorsed - not in such a positive way
as to necess. an admission or denial of
his candidacy from Albany himself - but
in general but not doubtful terms.
I am satisfied Easter & Bennett do
not want Albany endorsed but they
believe if Easter should be elected Ever or
more a good man he himself might be
named for Presid. or V. Presid. I told Albany
to go train Sept 21 and I think he is
satisfied that I does not want him
endorsed but that I may have secured
his followers into it.
I have at least forced the machine
out the open.
