18 An. Work 10.40 Am. Lord.
19 Thursday. Carlisle asked me to bring articles
back to Wells w. certain suggestions as to
slight modifications otherwise articles might
prejudice certain negotiations going on
bet. Eng. & U.S.
Saw Wells at Norwich; he agreed to make the
suggested corrections.
Went on to Boston to see Eva as to proposed
Bill in Lizell. auth. laying off Knox. on District
St.
Mar. 30 Left for N.Y. Saw Henry Huntz in Evening
at Reform Club; arranged with W. Lin &
Chas. Jackson in Boston as to collecting
funds for sound money.
" 31 In Boston. Made long speech before
Con" in RR's at legislative apart. Bin
erating new Union Station.
Apr. 1 In New York. Met Ned & Helen on S.S.
Magistre. Their homes were left by them
in Trust Benjamin.
" 7 Boston. Made another address on Union
Stn
" 13 & 14. Col. J.H. Benton Jr made official arguments on

The Bos.t. Com. Council unanimously
condemned Mayor Quincy's plan for a
new Union Station this last week.
April 18 Gov Russell this Judge Governor
announced himself as a Presidental
candidat. Said in papers that I have
been working for Carlisle & Russell.
Not him; I have kept absolutly out
of the matter not interfering with
certain Quincy friends nor Russell. I
have always, however, advised us
endorsing any one unless it could be
done with practical unanimity.
Beyond this I have done nothing.
Saw Disjourney Button in Boston. He
thought some one should see Quincy
and get author. from him to say that
(in case Russell was clearly going to
stampe the Convention) he had never
permitted the use of his name &
would not now. I felt that request
not to be done.
I saw Quincy April 17 & told him
of events: he assured if any organized
movement were being made in his
behalf. I still know of none.
