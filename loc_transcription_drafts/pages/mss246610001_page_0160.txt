McNary had hurriedly invited Easton
Running & myself to lunch at the Bellmore
but the meeting did not adjourn till
3 P.M. so we went to Exch. Club Humbled
w. me. Easton was not there but Id.
was also Fitzgerald, W.T.A., Dwyer,
McNary, & Hacketty.
We all agreed that an auxiliary
Corps to help Albany & carry on a
vigorous campaign in every long.
dist. was advisable -- that funds
also were needed. Agreed that Easton
Running & I should meet to arrange some
plan.

Running, W.T.A. Fitzgerald and myself.
