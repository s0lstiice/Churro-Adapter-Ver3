1914
Jan 19 Monday.
Atty. F. carried me up & said Arnold was fearful that
delay in trying up addict. civil settlement in
Washington might prejudice Ent case. I
told him of his interview and said I would
not even discuss a civil settlement unless
decided by him. From his manner & rather felt
that he wanted to determine the criminal
proceedings. I told him the Recorder and I
had gone over the matter and we could
not advise him in view of the record.
He asked me to call him up when I
left from Boston
Jan 20 In Boston. Dunham Tavern Club
Jan 22 Sunday
Called up Att'y En as requested. Told him of
my views. Winning womanizer ever and
said if he wished it I would send him
a copy. He said he would leave to see it
and would later discuss matter with
me.
I accordingly wrote him a letter on my
own hand sending the copy but had a
copy printed for my copy book.
Called with Mr. Harry Collins to meet Cardinal
Gibbons: Present: The Cardinal, C. Justice White
Judge Hughes, Gen. Bacon, Gen. DePettit, Col. Gen.
Aldrich, Shanks Ambasssador, Lindberg Berland
Ambassador, & wife of the Miss Clark (
Shearer Clark daughter) etc.
