regularly paid by the Consignees! He made
this statement when we were going over the
principal message, & though it was, in some
special message of Douglas's.
Nov. 3
wrote Judge Connell that, at his request I would
sign the Dolan's indorsement for Register of
Probate. I also signed a postal card
endorsing Dimision for Dist. Atty. In the
evening Mr. Cowley telephoned me from
the Mayor office asking if I would give
an interview for Dimision. I said "I shall
vote for Dimision and believe he will be
elected" and this was published in the
Sunday paper.
