Mr. Graham this day sent my letter resolution
to the Russian, Japanese & English Govts.
Mr. Dingley of Maine introduced Bill directing
Sec'y of Treasury to sell off all sales on the
Okinawa islands; refused to buy, v. H. Conn. I shut
a whole day with Conn. stated that to block
such a resolution if passed would be an abuse
of Paris awards & that before the third
could reach the islands the British would insist
on taking in the N. Pacific during the cloud
season. May join Dingley and assert they could
almost annihilate the hard labor at reached
the islands. I also stated that it was possible
that if the sales were all killed, the 6 or 10 million
dollars realized from the sale might in law be
held to belong to the N. Amer. Com. Co the license
of the exclusive right to kill seals on the Pit-
islands.
While on the floor of U.S. Senate this morning
Sen. Smith of N.J. in cheering of the Taft's Bill
told me that he persuaded Sen. Dibby to vote for the
Bill he had to loan him $7000. for which he
now held his notice. He immediately had
to vote for the Bill
