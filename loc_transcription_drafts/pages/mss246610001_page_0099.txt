24 Noon. Lat 48º 49' N
Long 126º 26' W.

& Smith, Marquis etc, late Prince
Min. of Japan during Chinese war,
Mr. Lisboa, wife, son & 2 daughters
Brazilian Minister to Japan just
on way from Russia; Mr. Porlowski-
Kosill and Mr. Michael Andrew

Mr. G. G. Peters Boston, A. W. Hoyt, N.Y.

24 Had talk with Marquis etc. found
him a very broad man - spoke with
much disfavor of the spirit of
improvisism among certain classes of
Japanese - they were in a large
minority. Shore most kindly of
N.S. - we had always been friendly
to Japan. Said he did not approve
of Japanese protest against annexa-
tion of Hawaii
