June 24
Senator Hickenlooper, acting Chairman of the Senate
Banking & Currency Committee called me at Kearny
about 11.30 am. He said he had read the
extract of my life and he wanted to say to
me that Charles E. Russell's book had been sent
the Senate -- he did not say by whom -- in which
I was attacked as having had a secret return
of 50000 from the Bank of Illinois RR. while
treating law in Boston, I told him fully as
to this, explained how it originated and show
that it was simply a political attack
as there was no secrecy about it whatever
He asked me the exact time during which
I was retired and I explained to him
fully as in the letter subsequently sent
to Sen. Hallie. He then hung up the telephone.
A little later Sen. Hallie called me up to
say the Court had voted 4 to 1 (he must
have meant 10 to 1 as Sen. Clifton is a Judge)
to support favorably my nomination after
carefully considering the Russell articles
and my reply. He said Sen. Breckinridge voted
against me because of these articles & that
he could have voted for me otherwise.
Hallie said -- on my request whether it would be
not be well to ask the Brandiers to file him -- that
this was an excellent idea & I at once
telephoned Brandiers to the Garden -- he said
he would see Breckinridge at once. I telephoned
he had seen him and he felt sure he could
not say he had completely disavowed
his opposition.
I then wrote Sen. Hallie a letter giving all
the facts as to my relationship with the
B.C.R.R. Committee & B. C. R.R. and sent it to him.
Left in train 4.03 PM for Boston, at 4.15
late night of June.
July 2
Evening. Met Sen. Breckinridge in N.Y. at 8 o'clock -- he
told me about some of Hallie's -- said
he had urged Breckinridge to vote for me
and Hallie confirmed this. Said he would
use every means to bring Breckinridge over, and
at one time he thought he would commit
me by giving me the opportunity to
go before the Court, but they were all --
except Breckinridge -- of opinion that as I was
in private life I had a right to leave
any case & were satisfied there was no
secret in my relations.
In letter to Sen. Hallie I clippings showing
attitude of Court in my money letters
from & against B.C.R.R.
