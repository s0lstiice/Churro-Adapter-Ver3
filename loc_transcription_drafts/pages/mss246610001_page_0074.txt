See. Carlisle told me at dinner that Almy
was opposed to having Judge Almy on the
Venezuelan Comm but that the President had
told him (C.) that he intended to appoint the
Comm w/out any special regard to Almy's
wishes. C. also said that if the President had
left his friends into council he never
would have put the threat of war into
his message; he implied that the President
had not consulted w. the cabinet at all
in the matter. C. thinks that while the
position taken in the message & counsel
is insurmountable yet the form of battle was
unfortunate & would have been expressed
w. just as much force in a more
diplomatic manner.

149
Had long talk with James Trecue Asst. at San
Francisco. He said he would see how the feeling
was towards Mr. Carlisle for President and
would write me.
Also had talk with Col. Bush Naval officer
- he said he was for Carlisle and that he
would leave charge of all California
matters with this end in view. He told him
I regarded him as our Rep. in California
for this purpose.
"16 Went to N.Y. to see Ned & Helen off on Fruit
Business. Also had interview w. Gustav
Schwab, Mr. Windmuller, Mr. Graham, and
other gentlemen representing Sound money
Comm. of Chamber of Commerce, as to the
best course to further interests of sound
money. This was a private trip and I
paid my own expenses. Spent day on Ellis
Island w. Harriet, Jane & Miss Sanford.
"17 Left for N.Y. (Sunday) to meet Asst. Burn
hannon to talk over civil matters comm.
