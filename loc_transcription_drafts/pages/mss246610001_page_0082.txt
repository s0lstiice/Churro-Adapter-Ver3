June 18
Breakfasted again w. Mr Tucker. drove
w. Sen. Lindsay & Harry Tucker to grave
of Gen. Stonewall Jackson thence 15
miles to the Natural Bridge. Thence to
Clifton Forge where we took midnight
train for Washington.
