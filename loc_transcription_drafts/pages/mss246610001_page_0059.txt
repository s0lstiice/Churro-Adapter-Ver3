April
14
1895
Capt Aetholin of Russian Navy called; he ex-
claimed that Russia & England appointed
Commissioners to set apart Afghanistan as
neutral territory between Russia & England
in wh. neither Nation should march.
He believed that some concession given by
England in this matter involved Russia
to sign a modern Sir. w. law, prescribing a
zone of only 30 miles around Russian
islands -- wh. was introduced into the
Am erican case to injure us before the tribunal.
Have dinner to Sec & Mrs Grosham -- Sec & Mrs
Carlisle, Mrs Burwell, Sir. Morton, Japanese
Minister, Mrs Lorimer, Miss Alice Lee & Col.
Atkinson. Dinner: crowded by Atkinson on
added in own. Total cost $2.75
21 Left for Boston to attend funeral of my
grandmother who died April 20 at nine wild
have been 75 yrs old in June next. Returned
to New York -- attended to some business
business there.
119
May
5
1895
Draw up detailed instructions for Capt Hooper
commanding the Rev. fleet to patrol Bering
Sea as to seizing vessels for violating American
Shoal draft to Secy Carlisle and Atty. Gen.
Along who obtained them & they were sent
to Hooper.
Left to N.Y. to confer with Collector as
to ordering reliquidation of all vessels during
August 1894 because of due of Cus. Ct N.Y.
that Tax. law was retroactive leaving effect
August 1 instead of Aug. 28. From there went
to Boston to confer with Bost. Collector as
to same.
came back this N.Y. confirmed w. Bd of
Gen. Assess and sent Mr. Gibson just about
counsel for Ent. before Board.
Reached Washington Thursday May 9.
Left w. Logan Carlisle for Phila -- went
w. Ins R. Read Collr Ch. E. Inspect. Assr. and
Gen. F. Harbity on Rev. cutter "Hamilton" for a
two days cruise down Delaware River
and outside. Arrived back Washington
Sunday evening May 12.
