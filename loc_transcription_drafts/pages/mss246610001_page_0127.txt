Sept 2 Quincy & Mc Connell dined w me at
Hotel Somerset. Q. went over whole
ground - said he would see if Easton
would withdraw for me - said that Q.
last March sent for Donovan & other
party leaders and asked their support
for the month for Gov. they told him
they could support him if Q. did not
want it. Q. then sent for Quincy
& Q. said he did not want to run &
would support Easton. Q. told us that
if he ran it would not be as a friend
of either Easton or myself but simply
to further best interests of the Party.
