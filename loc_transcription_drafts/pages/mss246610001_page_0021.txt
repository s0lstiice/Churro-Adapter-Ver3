Thomas Divine of Salem at a dinner at
which Mayor Rantoul, Alderman Philip
Little, Clifford Brigham, Purditt of Corn. Council
and others were present. Then we were
given a public reception at Essex House.
After rally Divine gave us another lecture
in the evening: we sat to bed about
2 A.M. Stayed at Mr. Divinis house.
