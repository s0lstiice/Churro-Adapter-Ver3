1903
Mar
23 Have been elected this year President of Man
Reform Club and Vice President of Man Civil Serv.
Reform Assoc'tin.
Robt Burnett telephoned the other day that he
wanted to talk with me as to some letters
he had received from Congressman favorable
to Mr. Always now in for Pres'dt. I accordingly
arranged him to lunch with me at the Exchange
Club which he did today. After a short talk
he said that he was very much interested
in Always now in and that he would gladly
collect funds for all me. expresses to facilitate
it. He then rather abruptly changed the
subject after saying that it was his
earnest belief that I should go to the
National Convention as Delegate at Large in
Always interest.
He said that Eastern mother was dying
with a stroke of apoplexy and that I
might decide not to run for Governor
again as it was largely to please his
Mother that he done it last year.
It was he said quite possible that
I might have to run -- that I was
always his second choice etc etc.
