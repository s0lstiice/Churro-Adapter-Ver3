Lunch Room
One room used in common by whites and
colored. By common consent the colored
took certain tables, entirely voluntarily. No
trouble at all
Discrimination in promotions
This discrimination, ordered by Mrs. Lee, wellknown,
has in punishment of order of C.D.O., been entirely
done away with and absolute justice in
promotions is now being accorded colored
employees.
